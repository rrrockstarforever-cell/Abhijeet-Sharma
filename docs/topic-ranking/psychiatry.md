# Psychiatry: ranked topics for NEET-PG and INI-CET

Sources and confidence: NBEMS and AIIMS publish no official topic-wise weightage. This ranking combines recalled past-paper patterns from coaching analyses (PrepLadder, GetOnCourse, DigiNerve, Careers360 subject notes) with general knowledge of how the exams weight psychiatry (roughly 4 to 8 questions per paper, mostly clinical vignettes).
Confidence: high for the top 15 ranks (they recur across every source), moderate for ranks 16 to 60, and low for the tail (ranks 61 onward), which is ordered by likelihood of appearing at all.
Tier key: Very high = recurs most years; High = several times in five years; Medium = occasional; Low = rare, one-liner or recall only.
