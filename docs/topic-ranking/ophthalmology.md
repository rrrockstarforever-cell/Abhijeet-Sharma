# Ophthalmology — NEET-PG and INI-CET topic ranking

Confidence: moderate for broad priority tiers, low for exact positions within a tier; this is an ordinal study guide, not an audited frequency count.
Scope: NEET-PG 2018–2025 and INI-CET from its November 2020 inception through 2025; 2019–2020 AIIMS-PG/JIPMER-PG provide predecessor context, not INI-CET papers.
Basis: recalled-paper knowledge and linked 2024–2025 recall/revision sources; recalls are incomplete, exam weighting varies, and lower rows provide syllabus coverage without claiming every topic appeared in this interval.
