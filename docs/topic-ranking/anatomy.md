# Anatomy: ranked topics for NEET-PG and INI-CET
Sources: coaching-site subject weightage and high-yield pages (DigiNerve, Careers360, GetOnCourse question banks, PrepLadder, CollegeDekho) found by web search in Nov 2026; no official NBEMS chapter-wise data exists, and the search gave no full multi-year PYQ tally for anatomy.
Confidence: the Very high and High tiers are fairly reliable (they match the usual pattern of brachial plexus, neuro, pelvis, heart, embryology and cross-sections). Exact ranks within a tier and the Low tier are estimates; verify against your own tagged PYQs before relying on the order.
Tier rule used: Very high = recurs almost every sitting; High = most sittings; Medium = a few per sitting; Low = rare.
