# Physiology: NEET-PG and INI-CET topic ranking

Sources: two web searches (Careers360, PrepLadder, PW, DigiNerve, CollegeDekho subject-wise and high-yield lists, 2026), which found no official NBEMS or AIIMS physiology breakdown and no year-by-year 2018-2025 physiology tally; plus standard knowledge of recurring PG physiology question patterns.
Confidence: LOW to MEDIUM. Tiers for the top ~30 topics (cardiovascular, renal, respiratory, endocrine, membrane/neuro basics) agree across sources; the exact order within a tier and the Medium/Low split are estimates, not counted data.
Verify before relying on it: tally the 2018-2025 NEET-PG and INI-CET papers topic by topic, then re-rank.
