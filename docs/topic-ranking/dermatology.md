# Dermatology and Venereology: NEET-PG and INI-CET topic ranking

**Sources:** Year-wise NEET-PG and INI-CET dermatology previous-year listings (getoncourse.ai), a November 2025 INI-CET recall analysis (doctutorials.com), and coaching revision pages. None gives a verified count of questions per topic.
**Confidence:** Medium for the top ranks (leprosy, STIs, scabies, bullous disease, psoriasis, SJS/TEN, steroid adverse effects), which recur in the sampled papers. Low for the lower ranks, which rest on standard exam-prep knowledge rather than frequency data.
**Method:** Ranks combine the sampled recall signals (STDs, scabies, topical steroid effects, leprosy reactions, drug eruptions, case-based visuals) with the usual weight of each topic in NEET-PG and INI-CET. Recall suggests about 10 to 13 dermatology questions per INI-CET paper, mostly short and image- or case-based.
