# Orthopedics: NEET-PG and INI-CET Topic Ranking

Confidence: moderate for broad priority groups; lower for exact adjacent ranks, which are an expert synthesis rather than audited question counts.
Scope: NEET-PG 2018–2025 and INI-CET 2020–2025 recall patterns; INI-CET began in 2020, so 2019 AIIMS-PG patterns provide predecessor context only.
Evidence: incomplete, unofficial recalls limit completeness; tiers combine recurrence and clinical weight, and lower ranks include syllabus coverage rather than proven appearances in every period.

Recall cross-checks: [NEET-PG high-yield topics, 2020–2024](https://image.prepladder.com/content/d7LQUczvXM2mo8ALoEMY1746434646.pdf), [INI-CET subject-wise recalled topics](https://www.prepladder.com/neet-pg-study-material/preparation-strategy/ini-cet-important-subject-wise-previous-year-questions-topics), and [INI-CET orthopedics recalls](https://www.prepladder.com/neet-pg-study-material/previous-years-question-papers/pyqs-in-orthopaedics-for-ini-cet). These support broad priorities, not numerical frequencies or an official exhaustive paper analysis. Ranking pools both examinations; individual sessions vary.
