# Obstetrics and Gynaecology: NEET-PG and INI-CET Topic Ranking

Confidence: moderate for tiers, lower for exact adjacent ranks; this is an estimated combined revision priority, not an audited frequency count of every paper.
Scope: NEET-PG 2018–2025 and INI-CET 2020–2025, with 2019 AIIMS-PG predecessor context; INI-CET itself did not run in 2019. Recall availability and wording vary.
Basis: recurring recall patterns and syllabus coverage, cross-checked against [NEET-PG 2025 recall](https://image.prepladder.com/content/NEET-PG-Recall-Questions-2025.docx.pdf) and [INI-CET November 2025 discussion](https://www.cerebellumacademy.com/ini-cet-nov-2025-obg-recall-quick-discussion-by-dr-raina-chawla/?amp=1); lower tiers include less consistently recalled syllabus topics, and no exact counts are claimed.
