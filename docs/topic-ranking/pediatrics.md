# Pediatrics: ranked topics for NEET-PG and INI-CET

Confidence: moderate for broad tiers, lower for exact adjacent ranks; this is a qualitative study-priority estimate, not an audited frequency count.
Scope: NEET-PG 2018–2025 and INI-CET 2020–2025, with AIIMS-PG 2019 as predecessor context; INI-CET did not exist in 2019.
Basis: recalled-paper patterns and syllabus coverage; recall sets are incomplete, overlap other subjects, and cannot establish every question or exact weightage.
