Sources: built from general knowledge of NEET-PG and INI-CET radiology papers plus a web search of coaching-site PYQ listings (no official NBEMS topic-wise breakdown was found). Confidence: moderate for the top-tier ordering, lower for exact placement of the middle and low tiers. Radiotherapy counts are estimates; one INI-CET prep site puts Radiodiagnosis and Radiotherapy at about 9 questions (4.5%), unverified.
Tier guide: Very high = asked almost every sitting, often several times. High = asked most years. Medium = asked occasionally, usually as a single recall question. Low = rare, mainly as a one-line recall.
