# Anesthesiology: ranked topics for NEET-PG and INI-CET

Sources: no official NBEMS or AIIMS topic weightage exists. Ranking uses standard PG anaesthesia syllabus coverage, recurring themes in coaching-site analyses of NEET-PG/INI-CET papers (Prepladder, Oncourse, Careers360, Diginerve), and general exam-pattern knowledge.
Confidence: high for the top ~20 (they recur across every source), moderate for the middle band, low for the exact order below rank ~50. Treat the tiers as a study guide, not a count from the papers.
Verify against your own tally of 5-8 years of PYQs before relying on the exact rank order.
