# Pathology: ranked exam topics for NEET-PG and INI-CET

Sources: no official NBEMS or AIIMS topic weightage exists. The ranking uses general knowledge of the standard pathology syllabus plus a few coaching-platform analyses (PrepLadder "Most Repeated Topics in NEET PG PYQs" and "INI-CET Question Trends 2026", DigiNerve pathology rapid-revision list, Careers360 INI-CET high-weightage list). The search returned no true topic-by-topic count from 2018-2025 papers, and the sources disagree on pathology question counts (roughly 10-20 per exam).
Confidence: moderate for the top 25 (haematology, neoplasia, inflammation, renal, CVS, CNS and general pathology are consistently named). Low for the exact order below rank 40, because it is an estimate, not a count. Verify against past papers before using it for planning.
Ranking basis: how often a topic appears in recalls and PYQs, weighted by how many marks it carries. Tier: Very high, High, Medium, Low.

## Notes on use
- Very high and High rows (ranks 1 to about 60) cover most of the marks in recent papers, based on the coaching analyses.
- Exact question counts per topic were not verified; treat the order as a planning guide.
- The INI-CET list leans further toward neoplasia, histopathology images, tumour markers, and haematological malignancies, so rank those higher when preparing for INI-CET.
