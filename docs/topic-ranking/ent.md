# ENT topic ranking — NEET-PG and INI-CET

Confidence: moderate for broad priority tiers; low for exact positions within a tier. This is an estimated revision ranking, not audited question counts.
Scope: NEET-PG 2018–2025 and INI-CET 2020–2025; 2019 refers to the predecessor AIIMS/JIPMER PG exams, since INI-CET began in 2020.
Basis: remembered recall patterns and published recall/high-yield resources; frequency, clinical importance and image-question potential inform ordering, with lower tiers completing syllabus coverage.
